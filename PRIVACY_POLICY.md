## 1. Introduction
VandeCoin ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting user privacy and ensuring full transparency regarding how data is handled. This Privacy Policy governs the use of the **VandeCoin (VDC)** mobile application distributed via the Google Play Store.

By creating a VandeID, signing in, or using VandeCoin, you acknowledge and agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect only the minimal data strictly required to deliver our participatory digital ecosystem:

### A. Information Provided by the User
- **Account Credentials:** When registering for a VandeID, we collect your email address and your chosen pioneer handle (e.g., `@VandePioneer`).
- **Profile Information:** Profile display name and avatar image URL.

### B. App Activity & Participation Metrics
- **Ecosystem Achievements:** Daily streaks, educational quiz results, unlocked achievement badges, and earned VDC participation points.
- **Trust Network (VandeCircle):** Direct peer connections established within the community trust circle.

### C. Technical & Diagnostic Information
- **Anonymous Telemetry:** Crash logs and performance diagnostics collected via Google Firebase Analytics to resolve crashes and improve application responsiveness.

---

## 3. Financial & Device Abuse Compliance (Zero-Hardware Eco-Friendly Model)
> **Google Play Financial Instruments & Device Abuse Policy Notice:**  
> VandeCoin does **NOT** engage in on-device cryptocurrency mining. The application does not execute hardware-intensive cryptographic Proof-of-Work algorithms, does not run persistent background processes that consume device CPU or drain battery life, and does not charge or transfer real-world fiat currency. All VDC tokens represent gamified ecosystem participation and educational achievement points.

---

## 4. How We Use Your Information
We use collected data solely for:
- Authenticating your identity securely across devices.
- Synchronizing your VDC balance, streaks, and quiz progress in real time via Cloud Firestore.
- Calculating and displaying opt-in global and community leaderboard rankings.
- Preventing duplicate registrations and automated bot fraud.

---

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers
Data is processed securely using enterprise cloud services provided by **Google Firebase (Google LLC)**:
- **Firebase Authentication:** Secure user identity management.
- **Cloud Firestore:** Real-time synchronized cloud database.
- **Firebase Analytics:** Aggregated anonymous application telemetry.

All data transmitted between your device and Google servers is encrypted in transit using modern TLS 1.3 encryption and encrypted at rest using AES-256.

---

## 6. User Data Rights & Mandatory Account Deletion
In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data Policy, users maintain complete control over their personal data:

- **Direct In-App Deletion:**  
  You can permanently delete your account, authentication record, streak history, and cached local storage at any time directly within the app:
  1. Open the **Profile** tab.
  2. Tap **Delete Account & Stored Data** under the *Account & Safety* section.
  3. Confirm the deletion prompt.
  *Upon confirmation, your Firebase Auth record and associated Cloud Firestore profile document are immediately and permanently erased.*

- **Manual Deletion Request:**  
  You may also request complete data removal by emailing **privacy@vandecoin.network** from your registered email address. Requests are processed within 48 hours.
